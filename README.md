Designed specifically for furniture retail businesses, this application helps streamline showroom sales, warehouse inventory, and customer management all in one place.

🛠️ Tech Stack: 
• Frontend: React.js, Vite
 • Backend: Node.js, Express.js
 • Database amp; ORM: MySQL, Prisma ORM

💡 Key Features: 
• POS checkout and sales management 
• Product amp; product-variant management 
• Dual inventory tracking for showroom and warehouse 
• Customer and installment management 
• Delivery management, sales history, and reporting 
• Built-in database transactions, stock validation, and automatic inventory deduction to 
prevent overselling 
• Automatic invoice generation, paid amount, and balance calculation
